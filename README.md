# Severity-and-Case-Classification-


1. What Is Case Classification?

Case classification is the process of categorizing an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) based on its seriousness, severity, validity, and other relevant criteria. It helps determine the appropriate handling, assessment, and reporting of adverse events.

Severity: Severity refers to the intensity of an adverse event, such as mild, moderate, or severe.

Seriousness: Seriousness is based on specific regulatory criteria, such as death, life-threatening conditions, hospitalization, disability, congenital anomaly, or other medically important events.

A severe event is not always a serious event. For example, a severe headache may not meet the regulatory criteria for a serious case.
